Purchase Tender

Compare every open RFQ offer, award one vendor.

One screen gathers, product by product, every offer sitting in an open request for quotation. The lowest price, the earliest arrival and the largest quantity are marked in green. Award a vendor and the competing lines clear themselves — no Purchase Agreement needed.

Odoo 20 Community & Enterprise Multi-currency comparison One-click award No Agreements dependency

Why this module

Without it

  • Open each RFQ one by one to find out who quoted what
  • Offers in different currencies compared by hand
  • The losing lines deleted manually, one order at a time
  • Nothing on record about why that vendor was chosen
  • Purchase Agreements or Alternatives installed just to compare offers

With Purchase Tender

  • Every open offer for a product on one screen, grouped by product
  • Prices converted to the company currency, both shown side by side
  • Choose clears the competing lines in one click
  • Each touched request gets a chatter note saying what happened
  • Standard Purchase only — purchase_requisition and purchase_alternative not required

Key features

Fills itself

A product appears in the tender as soon as two or more different open RFQs of the same company ask for it. Nothing to set up, no tender document to create.

The best offer is green

Lowest unit price, earliest expected arrival and largest quantity are highlighted per product. On a tie every matching row is marked.

Currencies compared properly

Each price is converted with the order's own rate, and the column shows both figures: 800.00 USD (10 120 000.00 UZS).

Award in one click

Choose gives the product to that RFQ. Every other RFQ loses its line for the same product; one left with no other line is cancelled instead of being left empty.

Drop a single offer

Cancel removes just that offer under the same rule and leaves the rest of the tender running.

Every decision on record

The winning request and each cleared one get a chatter message naming the product, the vendor, the quantity, the price and what was done.

Managers decide

Purchase users see the tender; only a Purchase Manager can award or drop an offer, enforced in the server method, not just hidden in the view.

Straight to the order

Clicking a row opens the request for quotation it belongs to, inside the Purchase app.

Only open requests

RFQ and RFQ Sent take part. A confirmed, cancelled or zero-quantity line leaves the tender on its own.

How it works

1

Ask several vendors

Create a request for quotation per vendor, the way you always do. The same product on two of them is enough.

2

Open Purchase → Tender

The offers are already there, grouped by product: vendor, expected arrival, unit price and quantity, one row per request.

3

Read the green

Cheapest, soonest and biggest are marked for you. Open any row to see the full request before deciding.

4

Choose the winner

Confirm, and the competing lines clear themselves. Confirm the winning RFQ as usual — it was never modified.

Screenshots

Purchase → Tender, grouped by product

Three products, five vendors, five ordinary requests for quotation — nothing entered twice. The green cells are the best offer in each column, picked per column, so one vendor does not have to win everything: for the office chair Deco Addict is cheapest and offers the most, Azure Interior arrives first. Azure Interior quotes in euros, and its price is shown with the company-currency value next to it.

Choose asks before it acts

Choose and Cancel are shown to a purchase manager only, and both say what will happen before anything changes.

The decision stays on record

The laptop was awarded to Gemini Furniture. Its request for quotation keeps its status and lines, and the chatter says what was awarded, at what price, and how many competing offers were cleared. Every other request that lost a line gets a note as well.

Prices are compared, not just listed

When vendors quote in different currencies, the smallest number is not the cheapest offer. Every price is converted to the company currency before the comparison, and the column shows the vendor’s own price so nobody has to trust a hidden conversion.
